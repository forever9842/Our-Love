The Flashback

1 August 2023 4:00PM

The Magic Day ✨

Wahi lamha jab mene apko pehli bar dekha tha. Aap sar jhukaye khamoshi se bethi thi. Chehre par hijab or masumiyat thi. Na aap muje janti thi na me aapko. Hum dono ek doosre ke liye bilkul anjan the. Magar shayad kuch mulaqate sirf ittefaq nahi hoti Us chhote se kamre ne hamari pehli nazar dekhi pehli baat suni pehli muskurahat mehsoos ki. Phir wahi kamra dheere dheere hamari yado ka ghar ban gaya tha. Har mulaqat ne ek naya panna likha. Har baat ne ek naya ehsaas diya. Bahut kuch badla… Bahut kuch guzra… Aur bahut kuch hum dono ke darmiyan hamesha ke liye reh gaya.Aaj bas ek baat kehna chahta hu, jo shayad aap nahi jaanti… Ye bilkul sach hai ki aapne mujhe chuna… aur ek behtar insaan bana diya.Lekin ek aur sach bhi hai. aap mujhe isliye chun paayi kyunki maine aapko mujhe chunne ki ijazat di thi.Aur maine ye ijazat isliye di… kyunki mujhe aapse mohabbat ho chuki thi. Haan… aapke izhaar se pehle me aapko mana bhi kiya tha. Lekin wo inkaar mohabbat ki kami nahi tha. Woh sirf ek darr tha… Darr is baat ka ki kahin meri wajah se aapko dard na mile. Mene apni zindagi me har woh cheez kho di thi jise mene dil se chaha tha. Isliye me ek qadam peeche hat gaya tha. Lekin dekho… aapki mohabbat ne mere har darr ko hara diya. Aaj jab peeche mudkar dekhta hu to lagta hai us din sirf do log nahi mile the… Us din do zindagiyan ek doosre ki kahani banne lagi thi. Aur shayad isi liye… 1 August sirf calendar ki ek tareekh nahi hai. Ye meri zindagi ka woh lamha hai, jahan meri duniya dubara jinda karke muje lai thi ❤️🌙

Late Nights

Late Night Talks 💬

Har mohabbat ki apni ek kahani hoti hai aur hamari kahani ki sabse khoobsurat yaade un raaton me basi hai Sardiyo ki woh lambi raate jab bahar thandi hawa chalti thi aur hum apni duniya me kho jaate the. Us waqt hum call par baat nahi karte the. Hamari puri duniya sirf SMS aur chat thi. Lekin un messages me itna pyaar itni bechaini aur itna apnapan hota tha ki waqt ka pata hi nahi chalta tha. Kabhi raat ke do baje… Kabhi teen baje… or kabhi kabhi puri raat... yaha tak ki kabhi baat karte karte subah tak ho jati thi. Neend aakho me hoti thi lekin dil sirf ek aur message ka intezar karta rehta tha. Phir waqt badla… Aur hamari zindagi me video call aa gayi. Sabse ajeeb baat ye thi ki hum aksar bolte hi nahi the. Bas ek doosre ko dekhte rehte the… Jese lafzon ki zarurat hi na ho. Kabhi aap muskurati thi… Kabhi me sirf tumhe dekhta rehta tha…Aur kabhi kabhi to hum video call chalu rakhte rakhte yun hi so jaate the. Na is baat ki fikr hoti thi ki koi dekh lega… Na is baat ka dar ki agar phone kisi apne ne utha liya to kya hoga. Us lamhe me sirf hum the… Haan, hamare darmiyan ladai bhi hui. or aksar hoti rahti hai Kabhi kabhi kai din tak baat nahi hoti thi. Lekin sach ye hai ki khamoshi sirf phone me hoti thi…Dil ke andar to har pal ek hi sawal hota tha… “Pata nahi woh kaisi hogi…” Aur aapke dil me bhi yahi sawal hota tha. Phir ek waqt aaya jab baat karna hamari aadat nahi hamari zarurat ban gaya. Aaj agar raat ko sone se pehle ek baar baat na ho to lagta hai jese din adhoora reh gaya ho. Neend bhi jaise ijazat maangti hai… “Pehle usse baat kar lo phir so jana.” Ab to haal ye hai ki khushi ho ya gham… Jeet ho ya thakan… Har ehsaas sabse pehle ek doosre ke saath baantne ka dil karta hai. Aur is note ke aakhir me… Me us phone ka bhi shukriya ada karna chahta hu. Ek aisa phone jo sirf ek device nahi tha… Balki hamare darmiya ek pul tha. Usne hume itni baaton me uljha diya ki aksar mehsoos hi nahi hota tha ki hum do alag jagahon par hain. Lagta tha… Jese hum ek hi kamre me ek hi khamoshi me ek hi muskurahat ke saath ek doosre ke saamne baithe hon. Shayad esi ka naam mohabbat hai… Jab fasle sirf nakshon me reh jaate hai aur dil har raat ek hi jagah milte hain. ❤️🌙

The Milestone

The Big 'YES' 💍

Log kehte hai ki har love story ki shuruwat tab hoti hai jab ek ladka himmat karke apne dil ki bat kehta hai aur ladki muskura kar “Yes” bol deti hai. Lekin hamari kahani bilkul alag thi… Hamari kahani me himmat tumne dikhayi thi. Sach kahu to us waqt muje samaj hi nahi aa raha tha ki kya karun. Dil tumhari taraf tha lekin dimaag muje rok raha tha. Mene apni zindagi me har us cheez ko khoya tha jise mene dil se chaha tha. Shayad isi liye muje mohabbat se zyada tumhare kho jane ka dar tha. Isliye mene tumhe “No” keh diya. Aaj bhi jab us pal ko yad karta hu to dil bhar aata hai. Lekin uske bad jo huwa usne meri puri zindagi badal di. Tum pehli baar mujse gale lagi or tum royi itna royi ki us waqt mere pas tumhe chup karane ke liye lafz hi nahi the. Aaj sochta hu wo aansu sirf aansu nahi the. Wo tumhari muhabbat thi tumhara yakeen tha tumhara dar tha ki kahin tum muje kho na do. Tumhari aankhon se girta huwa har ek aansu muje bas ek hi bat samja raha tha “Tum meri zindagi me kitni ahmiyat rakhte ho.” Us din muje pehli bar ehsaas huwa ki kisi ki mohabbat kitni sachchi ho sakti hai. Us din muje samajh aaya ki pyaar sirf “I Love You” keh dene ka naam nahi hota kabhi kabhi pyaar chup rehkar bhi sab kuch keh deta hai or tumhari khamoshi aur tumhare aansuon ne muje wo sab bata diya jo shayad lafz kabhi nahi bata paate. Waqt dheere dheere aage badhta gaya. Mera darr dheere dheere khatam hota gaya or meri har “No” dheere dheere ek hamesha ke “Yes” me badal gayi. Phir ek waqt aaya jab hume kisi proposal ki zarurat hi nahi rahi na kisi jawab ki na kisi wade ki q ki hum dono ko andar hi andar pata chal chuka tha ki ab ek dusre ke bina guzara mumkin hi nahi hai aaj jab peeche mudkar dekhta hu to lagta hai agar us din tum itni himmat na karti agar tumne apne dil ki bat na kahi hoti agar tumhare aansu muje meri ahmiyat na samjate to shayad aaj meri zindagi itni khoobsurat kabhi na hoti. Isliye meri nazar me hamari love story ki sabse khoobsurat yaad koi date nahi koi gift nahi… Koi photo nahi… Balki wo pal hai… Jab tumne apni mohabbat se mera har darr hara diya or us din se lekar aaj tak har din me Allah ka shukr ada karta hu ki usne meri zindagi me tum jesi mohabbat bheji. Because our forever didn’t begin with my proposal… It began with your courage… your tears… and your beautiful little “YES.” ❤️
